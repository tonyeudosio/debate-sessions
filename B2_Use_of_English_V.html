4 · Transformation patterns that keep coming up (Part 4)
PatternExample
must have + past participle (sure about the past)I'm sure she forgot. → She must have forgotten.
present perfect + for / sinceWe last met in 2020. → We haven't met for years.
present perfect continuousI started learning in 2023. → I have been learning since 2023.
Would you mind + -ingCould you open the window? → Would you mind opening the window?
passive with say / think / expectPeople expect it to rain. → It is expected to rain.
it + adjective + to-infinitiveIt is difficult to concentrate. → It is hard to concentrate.
Remember: between two and five words, and never change the word in capitals.
