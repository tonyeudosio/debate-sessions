4 · Transformation patterns that keep coming up (Part 4)
PatternExample
wish + past (a regret about now)It's a pity I can't swim. → I wish I could swim.
call off (= cancel)They cancelled the match. → The match was called off.
accused of + -ingThey think he took the money. → He was accused of taking the money.
had better (not)You shouldn't leave yet. → You had better not leave yet.
so → such a + adjective + nounThe book was so good. → It was such a good book.
prevent somebody from + -ingThe rain didn't stop us playing. → The rain did not prevent us from playing.
Remember: between two and five words, and never change the word in capitals.

To Learn Something, Teach It
It turns out that one of the best ways to understand something is to explain it to somebody else. Students 9 are asked to teach a topic remember it far better 10 those who simply reread their notes. The reason is simple: when you teach, you have to organise your ideas clearly, 11 forces your brain to fill in the gaps. There is 12 need for a real audience, either. In one study, people who merely expected to teach later performed better in tests, 13 though they never actually taught anyone. So 14 next time you prepare for an exam, try explaining the material to a friend, to your dog, or even to an empty chair. You will quickly discover how 15 you really understand.
