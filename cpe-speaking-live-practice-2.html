Assessment criteria — quick reference

For your own notes while listening, not read aloud to candidates.

Grammatical Resource

Range and control of structures; accuracy under pressure; ambitious forms attempted appropriately.

Lexical Resource

Range and precision of vocabulary; idiomatic and topic-appropriate word choice; paraphrasing when a word is missing.

Discourse Management

Coherent, extended contributions; relevant to the task; ideas well linked and developed, not a string of short answers.

Pronunciation

Individual sounds, word stress, sentence rhythm and intonation clear enough that a listener need not strain.

Interactive Communication

Initiating and responding, taking turns naturally, developing the conversation, negotiating with the partner.

Global Achievement

Overall effectiveness across the whole test — the examiner's holistic impression.
