Released in 1996 for the soundtrack of the film Phenomenon, "Change the World" became one of Eric Clapton's biggest worldwide hits. Produced by R&B legend Babyface, this acoustic pop-blues song earned three Grammy Awards in 1997, including Record of the Year and Song of the Year. Its smooth arrangement and soulful lyrics express an idealized, deep romantic longing.
